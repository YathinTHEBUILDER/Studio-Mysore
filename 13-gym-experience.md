# Gym Experience

## Purpose

The Gym Experience demonstrates how Studio Mysore can help gyms simplify membership enquiries, trial bookings and member onboarding through a modern digital experience.

The goal is to help gym owners imagine a system that supports both new members and staff while creating a professional first impression.

---

# Primary Goal

The visitor should leave thinking:

> "This would make joining my gym much easier."

and

> "This feels like something designed specifically for my gym."
