# Studio Mysore

## Project

**Yathin's Catalogue**

---

# Vision

Build a premium digital showroom where business owners can experience custom digital solutions before deciding to hire us.

Instead of telling clients what we can build, we let them experience it for themselves.

Every interaction should help them imagine how a solution designed specifically for their business could improve the way they work.

---

# Mission

Help local businesses discover better ways to run their business through thoughtful digital experiences.

Every website and system we build should solve real problems, feel effortless to use, and be designed around the way the business actually works.

---

# What We're Building

Yathin's Catalogue is an interactive website that demonstrates custom-built digital experiences for different industries.

The initial release includes experiences for:

* Cafés
* Restaurants
* Dental Clinics
* Medical Clinics
* Gyms

Each experience is fully interactive and demonstrates how a tailored solution could work for that business.

The website is not a portfolio.

It is a conversation starter.

---

# Primary Goal

Encourage business owners to start a WhatsApp conversation with Studio Mysore.

The website builds curiosity.

The experience builds trust.

The conversation closes the deal.

---

# Target Audience

Primary audience:

* Local cafés
* Restaurants
* Dental clinics
* Medical clinics
* Gyms

Future industries can be added using the same design system and experience framework.

---

# Core Principles

* Understand the business before proposing a solution.
* Show instead of telling.
* Keep everything simple and intentional.
* Design for usefulness before decoration.
* Every interaction should build trust.
* Every page should lead naturally to a conversation.

---

# Brand Personality

Studio Mysore should feel:

* Calm
* Premium
* Honest
* Thoughtful
* Approachable
* Detail-oriented

We never try to impress with complexity.

We earn trust through clarity and craftsmanship.

# Responsive Philosophy

The experience must feel intentionally designed for every device.

* Mobile
* Tablet
* Laptop
* Desktop

Responsive design means adapting the experience, not simply resizing the layout.

---

# Development Philosophy

Every feature should answer three questions:

1. Does this solve a real business problem?
2. Does it improve the visitor's experience?
3. Does it increase the likelihood of starting a conversation?

If the answer is no, it does not belong in the product.

---

# Project Structure

The project is divided into four layers.

1. Product Strategy
2. Design System
3. Experience Engine
4. Implementation

Each layer builds on the previous one to create a scalable and maintainable product.

# Definition of Success

A business owner should:

* Understand what Studio Mysore does within 30 seconds.
* Find an experience relevant to their business.
* Interact with at least one live demo.
* Feel that the solution is designed specifically for them.
* Start a WhatsApp conversation before leaving the website.
