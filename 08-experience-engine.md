# Experience Engine

## Purpose

The Experience Engine defines the structure followed by every industry experience.

Rather than designing each page independently, Studio Mysore uses one reusable storytelling framework that adapts to different businesses.

Every experience should feel unique while following the same underlying rhythm.

---

# Experience Philosophy

People understand products faster when they experience them instead of reading about them.

Every experience should answer one question:

**"What would this look like in my business?"**

---

# Experience Flow

Every industry page follows this sequence:

Hero

↓

Business Problem

↓

↓

---

# Section 01 — Hero

## Goal

Capture attention immediately.

Introduce the industry.

Create curiosity.

---

## Visitor Sees

Editorial photography.

Floating device.

Industry-specific atmosphere.

Large headline.

Minimal copy.

---

## Visitor Feels

Interested.

Curious.

Confident.

---

# Section 02 — Business Problem

## Goal

Show understanding.

The visitor should immediately recognise their own business.

---

## Rules

Talk about daily operations.

Avoid technical language.

Avoid software terminology.

Focus on business frustrations.

Examples:

* Too many phone calls.
* Missed bookings.
* Customers waiting.
* Manual work.
* Lost enquiries.

---

# Section 03 — Storytelling Scroll

## Goal

Show the solution visually.

Scrolling reveals the workflow.

Every animation should explain one idea.

---

## Rules

One idea per scroll section.

Never overwhelm.

Motion connects every step.

---

# Section 04 — Interactive Demo

## Goal

Create the "aha!" moment.

The visitor stops reading.

Starts interacting.

Starts imagining.

---

## Requirements

Zero confusion.

Instant feedback.

Works perfectly on touch devices.

Feels responsive.

No unnecessary complexity.

---

# Section 05 — Business Benefits

## Goal

Translate features into business value.

Never list software features.

Instead explain outcomes.

Examples:

* Customers wait less.
* Staff save time.
* Bookings become easier.
* Ordering becomes faster.
* Owners stay organised.

---

# Section 06 — Studio Mysore Process

## Goal

Reduce uncertainty.

Explain what happens after contact.

---

## Process

Understand

↓

Plan

↓

Design

↓

Build

↓

Support

Keep explanations simple.

---

# Section 07 — Conversation

## Goal

Start a WhatsApp conversation.

Nothing more.

---

## CTA

Let's talk about your business.

Supporting copy:

Tell us how your business works.

We'll suggest what we'd build and why.

# Hero Moment

Every experience receives exactly ONE unforgettable interaction:

* **Café**: QR ordering.
* **Restaurant**: Kitchen order flow.
* **Dental**: Appointment booking.
* **Clinic**: Patient check-in.
* **Gym**: Membership signup.

Everything else supports this moment.

---

# Motion Rules

Motion should:

* Guide.
* Explain.
* Confirm.
* Never distract.

The same motion language should be shared across all industries.

---

# Visual Rules

Every experience shares:

* Typography.
* Spacing.
* Layout.
* Component library.
* Navigation.
* Footer.

Only these elements change:

* Photography.
* Accent colour.
* Industry copy.
* Interactive demo.
* Hero animation.

---

# Copy Rules

Speak like a person.

Never sound like software documentation.

Never exaggerate.

Every sentence should help the business owner imagine using the product.

---

# Accessibility

Every experience must support:

* Keyboard navigation.
* Reduced motion.
* Touch interaction.
* High contrast.
* Screen readers.

---

# Performance

Target smooth performance on:

* Phone.
* Tablet.
* Laptop.
* Desktop.

Animations should never reduce usability.

---

# Experience Checklist

Before approving an experience:

* Is the business problem clear?
* Is the solution easy to understand?
* Does the visitor interact?
* Is there one memorable moment?
* Does it build trust?
* Does it naturally lead to WhatsApp?

If not, iterate.

---

# Decisions Made

* Shared storytelling framework.
* One hero moment per industry.
* WhatsApp-first conversion.
* Story-driven interaction.
* Component reuse across industries.
