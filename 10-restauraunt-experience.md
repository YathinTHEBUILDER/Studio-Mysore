# Restaurant Experience

## Purpose

The Restaurant Experience demonstrates how Studio Mysore can help restaurants create a smoother dining experience, simplify order management and strengthen direct customer relationships.

The objective is to help restaurant owners imagine a digital system designed around the way their restaurant operates.

---

# Primary Goal

The visitor should leave thinking:

> "This would make running my restaurant easier."

and

> "I want something built specifically for my restaurant."
