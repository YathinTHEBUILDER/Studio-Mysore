# Homepage Experience

## Purpose

The homepage is the first conversation between Studio Mysore and a business owner.

Its purpose is to create curiosity, demonstrate quality, build trust and guide visitors towards exploring an industry experience.

The homepage should never feel like a portfolio.

It should feel like an introduction to a better way of building digital products.

---

# Experience Goal

When a visitor leaves the homepage they should think:

> "I want to see what this would look like for my business."

---

# Success Criteria

The homepage is successful when visitors:

* Understand what Studio Mysore does.
* Feel the quality of the product.
* Choose an industry.
* Continue exploring.
* Start a WhatsApp conversation.

# Responsive Behaviour

## Mobile

Prioritise readability.

Stack layouts vertically.

Touch-first interactions.

Fast loading.

---

## Tablet

Use split layouts where appropriate.

Optimise for presentations during client meetings.

---

## Desktop

Enable richer storytelling.

Subtle cursor interactions.

Larger editorial layouts.

---

# Performance Goals

* Smooth scrolling.

* Instant interactions.

* Optimised images.

* Stable layout.

* Responsive animations.

The homepage should remain fluid across all supported devices.

---

# Accessibility

Keyboard accessible.

Visible focus states.

Reduced motion support.

High contrast.

Semantic HTML.

---

# Definition of Done

The homepage is complete when a visitor can:

* Understand Studio Mysore within 30 seconds.
* Navigate naturally.
* Experience at least one memorable interaction.
* Choose an industry.
* Feel confident enough to start a conversation.

---

# Decisions Made

* Homepage acts as an introduction, not a portfolio.
* One primary story before industry selection.
* WhatsApp remains the primary conversion path.
* One hero animation per page.
* Consistent responsive behaviour across devices.
