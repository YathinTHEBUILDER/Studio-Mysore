# Cafe Experience

## Purpose

The Café Experience demonstrates how Studio Mysore can help cafés create a faster, smoother and more enjoyable ordering experience.

The goal is not to sell software.

The goal is to help café owners imagine this experience inside their own café.

---

# Primary Goal

The visitor should leave thinking:

> "My customers would love this."

and

> "This would make my café easier to run."

---

# Hero

## Goal

Capture attention immediately.

Create curiosity.

Establish atmosphere.

---

## Headline

More time making coffee.

Less time taking orders.

---

## Supporting Copy

Let your customers browse the menu, place an order and pay in just a few taps.

# Scene 01 — The Problem

## Goal

Help café owners recognise familiar frustrations.

---

## Headline

Busy cafés shouldn't rely on busy counters.

---

## Problems

* Customers waiting to order.
* Staff repeatedly answering the same questions.
* Long queues during peak hours.
* Missed opportunities when the café is crowded.

---

## Visual

Simple illustrations are avoided.

Instead, use photography supported by subtle interface overlays.

---

# Scene 02 — Scroll Story

## Goal

Explain the customer journey visually.

---

### Step 1

Customer scans a QR code.

Animation: QR card appears naturally beside the phone.

---

### Step 2

Digital menu opens.

Categories slide into view.

Coffee images appear.

Everything feels immediate.

---

### Step 3

Customer selects drinks.

Cart updates instantly.

---

### Step 4

Order confirmation.

The café dashboard receives the order.

---

### Step 5

Staff prepares coffee.

Owner sees the workflow complete.

---

## Visitor Learns

Ordering can happen without waiting at the counter.

---

# Scene 03 — Interactive Demo

## Goal

Create the "aha!" moment.

---

## Interaction

Visitors can:

* Browse menu.
* Add items.
* Adjust quantity.
* View cart.
* Place a demonstration order.

---

## Response

Order confirmation appears instantly.

The owner dashboard updates.

The experience feels real.

---

## Hero Moment

The order appears on the café dashboard immediately after confirmation.

This should become the most memorable interaction on the page.
